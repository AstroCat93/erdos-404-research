Round 3 literature gate for Erdős #404 — verified references (2026-10-12).
Verification = bibliographic record seen at the named URL (publisher page, Project Euclid, arXiv abstract, Numdam, Mathnet, IMPAN) via read-only fetch/search. "Content" lines are from abstracts only unless stated; no full texts were read.

(a) BPRE
A1. W. L. Smith, W. E. Wilkinson, On branching processes in random environments, Ann. Math. Statist. 40 (1969), no. 3, 814–827. doi:10.1214/aoms/1177697589 [Project Euclid]. i.i.d. environments; extinction a.s. iff E log m(ξ) <= 0 (plus integrability).
A2. K. B. Athreya, S. Karlin, On branching processes with random environments I: Extinction probabilities, Ann. Math. Statist. 42 (1971), no. 5, 1499–1520, doi:10.1214/aoms/1177693150; II: Limit theorems, 42 (1971), no. 6, 1843–1858 [Project Euclid]. Stationary ergodic environments. Announcement: Bull. AMS 76 (1970), 865–870.
A3. D. Tanny, Limit theorems for branching processes in a random environment, Ann. Probab. 5 (1977), no. 1, 100–116, doi:10.1214/aop/1176995894 [Project Euclid]. Explosion/extinction dichotomy.
A4. J. Coffey, D. Tanny, A necessary and sufficient condition for noncertain extinction of a branching process in a random environment (BPRE), Stoch. Proc. Appl. 16 (1984), no. 2, 189–197 [RePEc/ScienceDirect].
A5. D. Tanny, A necessary and sufficient condition for a branching process in a random environment to grow like the product of its means, Stoch. Proc. Appl. 28 (1988), no. 1, 123–139 [RePEc]. The Kesten–Stigum (Z log Z) analogue for BPRE (i.i.d. env.).
A6. H. Kesten, B. P. Stigum, A limit theorem for multidimensional Galton–Watson processes, Ann. Math. Statist. 37 (1966), 1211–1223, doi:10.1214/aoms/1177699266 [Project Euclid]. (Classical, fixed environment.)
A7. H. Kesten, M. V. Kozlov, F. Spitzer, A limit law for random walk in a random environment, Compositio Math. 30 (1975), no. 2, 145–168 [Numdam, MR 380998]. RWRE, not BPRE: stable limit law for hitting times; relevant only via the associated-walk heuristics.
A8. M. V. Kozlov, On the asymptotic behavior of the probability of non-extinction for critical branching processes in a random environment, Teor. Veroyatnost. i Primenen. 21 (1976), no. 4, 813–825; Theory Probab. Appl. 21 (1977), no. 4, 791–804 [Mathnet tvp3425]. P(T>n) ≍ n^{-1/2}; exact ~ c n^{-1/2} for linear-fractional offspring.
A9. J. Geiger, G. Kersting, The survival probability of a critical branching process in a random environment, Teor. Veroyatnost. i Primenen. 45 (2000), no. 3, 607–615; Theory Probab. Appl. 45 (2001), no. 3, 517–525, doi:10.4213/tvp491 [Mathnet]. Kozlov's n^{-1/2} for general i.i.d. offspring laws.
A10. V. I. Afanasyev, J. Geiger, G. Kersting, V. A. Vatutin, Criticality for branching processes in random environment, Ann. Probab. 33 (2005), no. 2, 645–673, doi:10.1214/009117904000000928; arXiv:math/0503657 [Project Euclid, arXiv]. Oscillating associated walk under Spitzer's condition; survival asymptotics and conditional FLTs.
A11. G. Kersting, V. Vatutin, Discrete Time Branching Processes in Random Environment, ISTE/Wiley, 2017, ISBN 9781786302526 [ISTE page]. Textbook; quenched and annealed critical case, three subcritical regimes.
Not verified: "Afanasyev 1980, Theory Probab. Appl. 25" — the only record found is Afanasyev's 1980 MSU candidate dissertation "Limit theorems for a conditional random walk and some applications" (cited in Geiger–Kersting–Vatutin 2003). Do not cite as a journal paper.

(b) random p-adic polynomials / p-adic Kac–Rice
B1. S. N. Evans, The expected number of zeros of a random system of p-adic polynomials, Electron. Commun. Probab. 11 (2006), 278–290, doi:10.1214/ECP.v11-1230; arXiv:math/0602478 [EJP-ECP, Project Euclid]. First p-adic Kac–Rice/co-area formula; expected count (1+p^{-1}+...+p^{-d})^{-1} p^{d floor(log_p N)}.
B2. J. Buhler, D. Goldstein, D. Moews, J. Rosenberg, The probability that a random monic p-adic polynomial splits, Experiment. Math. 15 (2006), no. 1, 21–32; arXiv:math/0404057 [Project Euclid]. Probability of full splitting over a complete DVR; recursion.
B3. M. Bhargava, J. Cremona, T. Fisher, S. Gajović, The density of polynomials of degree n over Z_p having exactly r roots in Q_p, Proc. London Math. Soc. 124 (2022), no. 5, doi:10.1112/plms.12438; arXiv:2101.09590 [Warwick WRAP 164111, Cambridge repository; page ranges disagree between repositories (468–478 vs 713–736): verify at publisher]. Densities are rational in p, invariant under p -> 1/p; expected number of roots of monic f is 1 (talk abstracts).
B4. X. Caruso, Where are the zeroes of a random p-adic polynomial?, Forum Math. Sigma 10 (2022), Paper e55, 41 pp.; arXiv:2110.03942; published online 18 Jul 2022 [Cambridge Core listing; DOI not seen]. p-adic Kac–Rice reformulation; distribution of roots over extensions; root repulsion.
B5. A. Kulkarni, A. Lerario, p-adic integral geometry, SIAM J. Appl. Algebra Geom. 5 (2021), no. 1, 28–59, doi:10.1137/19M1284737; arXiv:1908.04775. Integral-geometry formula over Q_p; random p-adic systems.
Gap: no paper found treating zero counts of random p-adic *power series* (as opposed to polynomials) or of random series with factorial-type coefficients.

(c) n! mod p, mod p^k, Morita Gamma_p
C1. C. Cobeli, M. Vâjâitu, A. Zaharescu, The sequence n! (mod p), J. Ramanujan Math. Soc. 15 (2000), no. 2, 135–154 [Illinois Experts; Vâjâitu's CV lists pp. 71–90 — verify]. Guy's question; residues missed ~ p/e like a random sequence.
C2. M. Z. Garaev, F. Luca, I. E. Shparlinski, Character sums and congruences with n!, Trans. Amer. Math. Soc. 356 (2004), 5089–5102; arXiv:math/0403422 [arXiv abstract; Integers reference lists].
C3. M. Z. Garaev, F. Luca, I. E. Shparlinski, Exponential sums and congruences with factorials, J. reine angew. Math. 584 (2005), 29–44; arXiv:math/0403424 [Macquarie research portal; arXiv]. NOTE: note.md's reference list conflates C2 and C3 (title of C2 with venue/arXiv of C3). Fix.
C4. O. Klurman, M. Munsch, Distribution of factorials modulo p, J. Théor. Nombres Bordeaux 29 (2017), no. 1, 169–177, doi:10.5802/jtnb.974; arXiv:1505.01198 [Centre Mersenne, Numdam]. Lower bound on average (over p) for number of residue classes missed by n! mod p.
C5. C. Cobeli, I. Pitrop (?), A. Zaharescu, Non-equal neighboring model for factorials (mod p), Filomat 38 (2024), no. 13 [pmf.ni.ac.rs]; C. Cobeli, A. Zaharescu, Factorials (mod p) and the average of modular mappings, arXiv:2011.07582. (Author list of the Filomat paper not independently verified.)
C6. Y. Morita, A p-adic analogue of the Γ-function, J. Fac. Sci. Univ. Tokyo Sect. IA 22 (1975), 255–266 [standard; not re-verified beyond Wikipedia/Robert GTM 198 Ch. 7].
Gap: no result found on n! mod p^k (k>=2) distribution, nor on equidistribution of Γ_p values / unit parts of n! in Z_p^×. Garaev–Luca–Shparlinski and Klurman–Munsch are mod-p only.

(d) sums of factorials, Kurepa
D1. S. Lin, On two problems of Erdős concerning sums of distinct factorials, Bell Laboratories internal memorandum, 1960 [erdosproblems.com/404 and /403 citation [Li76]; not online].
D2. M. Andrejić, M. Tatarevic, Searching for a counterexample to Kurepa's conjecture, Math. Comp. 85 (2016), no. 302, 3061–3068; arXiv:1409.0800 [AMS page]. p < 2^34.
D3. M. Andrejić, A. Bostan, M. Tatarevic, Improved algorithms for left factorial residues, Inform. Process. Lett. 167 (2021) 106078; arXiv:1904.09196. p < 2^40. Kurepa open as of 2021.
D4. Ž. Mijajlović, Fifty years of Kurepa's !n hypothesis, Bull. Cl. Sci. Math. Nat. Sci. Math. 46 (2021) [geodesic.mathdoc.fr BASS_2021_46_a9]. Status: open.
D5. V. Abramov, Kurepa's conjecture on the left factorial function is true, arXiv:2209.12677 (2022). Claimed proof; no journal publication, review, or refutation found. Treat as unverified.
D6. B. Dragovich, On some finite sums with factorials, Facta Univ. Ser. Math. Inform. 14 (1999), 1–10; arXiv:math/0404487. Equivalents of Kurepa; also Dragovich–Mišić arXiv:1411.4195 and Dragovich–Khrennikov–Mišić Filomat 31 (2017) on p-adic invariant summation of factorial series (Σ n·n! = −1 in every Z_p; Schikhof's question on irrationality of Σ n! is open, per Murty–Sumner notes).
D7. S. D. Hughes, Sums of distinct divisors of factorials, arXiv:2609.10902 (9 Sep 2026). Adjacent (divisors of n!, not factorials); no implication.
D8. T. Edgar, J. Integer Seq. 23 (2020/2023) on factorial-base representation (cites Lenstra's "Profinite Fibonacci numbers"-style carry rules). Weak relevance to Prop. 10.21.
No paper on p-adic valuations of sums of distinct factorials found (arXiv API: "distinct factorials", "sums of factorials", Erdős+factorial+p-adic; latest hits 2026-10-01 Yudin 2610.01899, unrelated).

(e) purity (Jessen–Wintner)
E1. B. Jessen, A. Wintner, Distribution functions and the Riemann zeta function, Trans. Amer. Math. Soc. 38 (1935), 48–88 [AMS page]. Purity law on R.
E2. P. Hartman, Infinite convolutions on locally compact Abelian groups and additive functions, Trans. Amer. Math. Soc. 214 (1975), 215–231 [AMS page]. Abstract: Lévy atom theorem and "pure theorems" of Jessen–Wintner–van Kampen for infinite convolutions on arbitrary LCA groups (hence Z_p). This is the citation for the p-adic Jessen–Wintner law used in §3.7(c). Full text not read; check that the pure-type statement covers general G, not only R^d.

(f) second moment
F1. J.-P. Kahane, Some Random Series of Functions, 2nd ed., Cambridge Stud. Adv. Math. 5, CUP 1985 (1st ed. Heath 1968) [CiNii]. Paley–Zygmund inequality: P(Z >= θ E Z) >= (1−θ)^2 (E Z)^2 / E Z^2.
F2. R. Lyons, R. Pemantle, Y. Peres, Conceptual proofs of L log L criteria for mean behavior of branching processes, Ann. Probab. 23 (1995), 1125–1138; arXiv:math/0404083. Size-biasing proof of Kesten–Stigum; the natural template for an L^2/size-biased argument for μ(K_p) > 0.

(g) exponential sums mod p^k
G1. L.-K. Hua, Additive Theory of Prime Numbers, Transl. Math. Monogr. 13, AMS 1965 [AMS bookstore]. Complete sums mod p^l via multiplicity of roots of p^{-t} f' (Hua's lemma).
G2. J. H. Loxton, R. C. Vaughan, The estimation of complete exponential sums, Canad. Math. Bull. 28 (1985), no. 4, 440–454 [Cambridge Core]. |S(f;q)| <= C_f d_n(q) q^{e/(e+1)}.
G3. T. Cochrane, Z. Zheng, Pure and mixed exponential sums, Acta Arith. 91 (1999), no. 3, 249–278, doi:10.4064/aa-91-3-249-278 [IMPAN]. Stationary-phase style evaluation mod p^m (per later citing papers).
G4. T. Cochrane, Exponential sums modulo prime powers, Acta Arith. 101 (2002), no. 2, 131–149, doi:10.4064/aa101-2-5 [IMPAN].
G5. T. Cochrane, Z. Zheng, Upper bounds on a two-term exponential sum, Sci. China Ser. A 44 (2001), 1003–1015, doi:10.1007/BF02878976.
G6. W. K. A. Loh, On Hua's lemma, Bull. Austral. Math. Soc. 50 (1994) [Cambridge Core]; J. H. H. Chalk, On Hua's estimates for exponential sums, Mathematika 34 (1987).
Caveat: these treat polynomial phases; the #404 Fourier coefficients (products of cos(π u n!/p^k)) are not polynomial exponential sums, so (g) is at best a template.
